LIBRARY POLICIES

Borrowing Guidelines & Hours

Everything you need to know about using campus library resources.

🕒

Operating Schedule

Open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM. Closed on official university holidays and weekends.

📚

Borrowing Privileges

Enrolled students may borrow up to 3 physical books at a time for a standard loan period of 14 calendar days.

🔄

Renewals & Extensions

Books may be renewed once for an additional 7 days, provided no other student has placed a hold or reservation.

⚠️

Care & Timely Returns

Please handle all materials with care. Prompt returns ensure equal access to required textbooks for all peers.
